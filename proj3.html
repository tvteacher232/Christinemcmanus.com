Project 3 Video Podcast

For this project, I created an animated open for the video podcast in Adobe After Effects. I then exported the animated open as a 1920 x 1080p video file.

I then created a audio loop on my Mac in Garage band with a cheering soundeffect and exported it to disk as a mp3. I uploaded the file to my Dropbox account to move it to my Windows machine.

I then used Adobe Captivate 2019 to create a webcam intoduction and closing with myself on video. I then recorded two application screen videos in Microsoft PowerPoint and one video in Adobe Photoshop. I exported all the videos to a video podcast folder.

Then I imported all the media files into Adobe Premiere Pro. I assembled all of the media on the new sequesnce and exported it as the Final Video Podcast - Christine McManus.mp4 file.

I then added the video to my media subfolder in the root folder of my portfolio website. Next, in Dreamweaver CS6, I embedded the video using the following HTML code:

<video width="620" height="440" controls>
<source src="media/Final Video Podcast - Christine McManus.mp4" type="video/mp4">
<source src="movie.ogg" type="video/ogg">
Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>
