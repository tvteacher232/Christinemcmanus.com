Project 2

For this project, I added an existing video I produced for my Introduction to Web Design course on Web Root Folders.

I first imported the video into Adobe Premiere CS6 (PR) placing the video on the V1 and A1 tracks. To keep the video size small for the web, I matched the I matched the video size of
640 x 360 with a frame rate of 25 frames per second.

I then imported the png version of my CM logo into PR and placed it on the V2 track. Since it was a png file, it automatically held its alpha channel creating the transparency. However, the logo filled the entire visual screen, so I opened the Effect Controls and changed the scale to 9. I also moved the position of the logo to x=571 and y=290.

Next, I saved the project in my classfolder for module 3 as Web Root Folder. I then exported the video by selecting File>Export>Media (with the timeline selected). I adjusted the following settings; Format = H.264, Peset=HD 1080P 29.97, and Output name=Web Root Folders - CM.mp4 (setting the location to the same as the project). I then selected Export and my new branded video was created.

I then added the video to my media subfolder in the root folder of my portfolio website. Next, in Dreamweaver CS6, I embedded the video using the following HTML code:

<video width="620" height="440" controls>
<source src="media/Web Root Folders - CM.mp4" type="video/mp4">
<source src="movie.ogg" type="video/ogg">
Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>
