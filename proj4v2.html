Project 4 - Version 2

I revised the original Project 4 version 1Homework Help Lesson in Adobe Captivate 2019. Here are all of the adjustments that I made in Version 2.

  • The introduction and conclusion music and sound effects were too long. Therefore, I determined they were not nescessary and removed them.

  • I developed new index tabs on the left side of the screen in Captivate. This will help my students to move through the 2 calculations.

  • I removed the section with only instructions and added those items into the demonstration slides.

  • On the page where students calculate slope, I developed a tab system. This helped to clarify the steps students would follow.

  • I added the equation to the top of the screen with the graph.

  • I removed the use of shapes for the equations.

  • I changed my voice to be more conversational in the tutorial.  I also slowed down my delivery.

  • I changed the stars next to the rise and run, replacing them with arrows to indicate the movement
    (rise – upward arrow and run- right facing arrow).

  • I included my email address on the presentation.

  • I added intermediate feedback on the assessment slides.

  • I added an additional multiple-choice selection on the final assessment question.
