Project 4 - Version 1

I created the following Homework Help Lesson in Adobe Captivate 2019.

For Project 4, I created a powerpoint of basic instructions to assist students in completing their pre-algebra math homework. Then I imported it into Adobe Captivate 2019. I added music, a voice-over, animations, and assessment that either pushes a student forward with correct answers or repeats the slide that answers the question so they can try again.
