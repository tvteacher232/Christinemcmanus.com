Teaching
& Learning

As an educator, I began teaching Early Childhood Education in October 2007. After years of additional training and education,
I switched to Career and Technical Education at the secondary and post-secondary levels, then to Middle School Mathematics, and finally to instructional coach training and mentoring a staff of over 100.

My brick-and-mortar schools (Roy Martin Middle School and ITT Technical Institute) and my online schools (Virtual High School, Nevada Connections Academy, Nevada Virtual Charter School, ITT Technical Institute, and RPDP through Southern Utah University) provided me with extensive experience in pedagogical, classroom, and content management skills.

The Canvas LMS system allowed me to flip learning in a 1-to-1 iPad school. Adobe Captivate allowed me to create self-directed learning modules for students with built-in assessment. Acrobat helped me create step-by-step tutorials for instructional materials. At various institutions, I worked with Blackboard, Edmodo, Canvas, and Google Classroom Connect to evolve instructional content beyond the paper worksheets found in a traditional classroom.

Now in my PhD, my professional goal is to continuously communicate with teachers and students, assisting them in achieving their academic goals. I seek to develop and research Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math applications and implementations in brick-and-mortar, blended, and online instruction through instructional design, augmented reality, virtual reality, and web-based technologies to meet the needs of educators and lifelong learners. I am passionate about seeking equity in user access by providing differentiation for all users, regardless of individual differences, ability levels, and socioeconomic status.
