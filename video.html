Video and Audio Production

With my BA in Communications from UNLV, I began an extensive career in the video production industry.  I first worked in broadcast television stations then later moved to the industrial television doing keynote presentations and conventions.  

I have taught secondary and post-secondary students various video skills including videography, journalism, lighting, directing, technical directing, producing and on air talent development.   I am an exceptional video editor with experience in Avid Media Composer, Final Cut Pro, iMovie, and Premiere. I can create stunning intros and web elements in After Effects.
