Computer Science

Secondary and Post Secondary Implementation and Instruction

During my time at Emporia State University, I collaborated with the Science and Math Education Center to design and develop a 360 Degree Virtual Tour of the Johnston Geology Museum. This project served as my Graduate Project in the Instructional Design and Technology Master of Science Program.

Capturing multiple 360-degree images, I stitched them together using Adobe Captivate 2019's Virtual Reality Project tool. When the project was complete, it was presented at the Graduate College Fair, then uploaded to the University's website.

During my time at Greyhound Exhibition Services (GES) as a Logistics Traffic Control Analyst (07/1999 – 03/2001), I developed a
Microsoft Access data tracking program that allowed nationwide locations to upload their Excel Logistics Orders into the system. This moved the system from a paper/faxed system to an online trackable system. Staff could plan and track shipments for customers from convention centers across the nation to their delivery locations. After delivery, when invoices were sent to Accounts Payable, staff could communicate that information to the shipping companies. This new system increased department efficiency and accountability.

As an instructional coach at Nevada Virtual Charter School, I create multiple data trackers in Google Spreadsheets and back-end code them using Google Apps Script. I am most proud of the NVCS House Points Tracker. It is a system staff uses to track and enter spreadsheets with house points students earned for multiple reasons every week. I designed it to allow staff to enter a single student independently or upload a tracking sheet with multiple class tabs, importing multiple students rapidly. board that displays the six houses and the live point values. I saved the tab as an HTML Page that dynamically updates as the points are entered. This is embedded in a PowerPoint Presentation in our Lobby on the NVCS Campus and embedded in each of the House Canvas Tiles on their Home Page.
This year, I modified the system to automatically email the student and their Canvas observer (parent), including the reason they earned the points.

I completed the Southern Nevada Regional Professional Development Program and earned my Introduction and Advanced Computer teaching endorsements in 2011. Then SNRPD hired me to teach the Python and Java for Educator graduate courses online at Southern Utah University (50% of the state endorsement program). I successfully instructed 2 cohorts through the programs in Spring 2025 and 2026.
